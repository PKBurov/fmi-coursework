# FMI courses — archive of my Moodle courses (learn.fmi.uni-sofia.bg)

Downloaded 20 Oct 2026 with the Moodle mobile web service. Layout: `<academic year>/<semester>/<course> [moodle id]/<section>/files`.

Each course folder also has `links.txt` (external links the course pointed to, e.g. YouTube lectures, GitHub) and `Форум - <name>/posts.md` with all forum posts plus their attachments.
